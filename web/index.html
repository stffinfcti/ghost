Ghost

Two $8 Wi-Fi boards that notice when someone falls and stays down, and text you. No camera, no wearable. One board sends a steady Wi-Fi signal, the other listens to how your body bends it.

1. Flash the boards

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Phones can't flash boards. Plug one board in with a USB cable that carries data (some cables only charge).

No board shows up in the list? It needs a USB driver: CP210x from Silicon Labs or CH340, depending on the small chip next to the USB port. If it still won't connect, hold the BOOT button while you press Install.

2. Put them on your Wi-Fi

  1. Power each board from any USB charger.
  2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network called Ghost-sender-…, pick your home Wi-Fi, type its password, Save.
  3. Do the same for Ghost-listener-…, and give the room a name.
  4. Your home Wi-Fi must be 2.4 GHz (most routers have both).

Hold the BOOT button for 3 seconds to make a board forget its Wi-Fi.

3. Set up the room

  1. Put the two boards on opposite sides of the room, about waist height.
  2. On a phone on the same Wi-Fi, open http://ghost.local (or the address the listener shows).
  3. Leave the room empty and press Calibrate. It takes 30 seconds.
  4. Install the free ntfy app and subscribe to the topic shown on that page. Press Send test alert to check.

Watch live from anywhere

Type the topic from the listener page. This shows the room's state over the internet.

Not watching

Ghost is a hackathon project and not a medical device. Don't rely on it as your only safety alert.
